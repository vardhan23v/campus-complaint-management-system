Before you submit

Good complaints get fixed faster

  • Be specific about the spot. "Third bench from the window, Room 112" beats "classroom".
  • One problem per complaint. Different problems often go to different technicians.
  • Add a photo of the damage if you can do so safely.
  • Emergencies first. For fire, sparking or flooding, alert security in person, then file the complaint.

This desk handles campus facilities and services only — not police, legal or public grievances.
